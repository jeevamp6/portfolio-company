Our Pledge

How Aegrion Protects Your Data

Data Isolation & Encryption

Customer telemetry and security event logs are logically isolated per organization. All data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols.

Least-Privilege Employee Access

Aegrion employees only access production systems through hardware-backed FIDO2 MFA keys, Just-In-Time role assumptions, and recorded audit sessions.

Vulnerability Disclosure Program

We maintain an active responsible vulnerability disclosure channel and collaborate openly with independent security researchers.

Continuous Compliance Alignment

Our infrastructure and operational processes are architected in strict alignment with SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and CIS Benchmark security controls.

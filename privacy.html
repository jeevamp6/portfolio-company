Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 4, 2026

Aegrion Security values your privacy. This policy details how we handle the personal information collected when you use our website, consult with our team, or apply for open roles.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information directly from you when you submit forms on our website. This includes:

  • Consultation Request Form: Your name, company name, corporate email address, contact phone number, type of service requested, and any specific messages or project details you share.
  • Career Application Form: Your name, email address, role of interest, portfolio URL, resume link (e.g., hosted on Google Drive or GitHub), and a custom cover note.

How We Store and Use Data

Your submitted data is stored securely using Supabase. We restrict access to this database strictly to authorized Aegrion Security administrators and hiring coordinators. We use this data only to:

  • Respond to your service requests and schedule consultations.
  • Evaluate and process your application for career opportunities at Aegrion.

Selling of Data

We do not sell, rent, lease, or share your personal information with any third-party marketing companies, advertisers, or other organizations. Your information is strictly used for Aegrion Security business communications and recruitment.

Cookie Usage & Preferences

We use cookies to run our site, remember your settings, and analyze site performance. We respect your preferences and provide a complete consent system allowing you to disable functional or analytical cookies. Strictly necessary cookies are active by default to ensure basic website functionality.

Data Retention & Deletion Rights

We retain consultation inquiries and job application records for as long as necessary to conduct business operations or evaluate hiring pipelines. You have the right to request access to, correction of, or complete deletion of your personal data from our systems at any time. To make a request, contact us via the details below.
